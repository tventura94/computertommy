Most local businesses here don't need a complex website — they need one that loads fast, explains what they do, and makes it easy to get in touch. That's what I build.

Whether you run a contracting company, tattoo studio, restaurant, therapy practice, or any other service-based business in the Asheville area, the approach is the same: figure out what your customers need to see, then build the simplest site that gets them to take action.

## What I Build

A typical project includes a clean homepage, focused service pages, an about section that builds trust, and a contact page with a form, phone number, and clear next step. That structure alone outperforms most template sites because it removes friction and answers the right questions upfront.

If you need service-area pages, neighborhood-specific landing pages, or tighter copy around the work you actually do, I can build those too. Everything is custom — no drag-and-drop builders, no bloated themes, no monthly platform fees.

For businesses that already have a site, I can also clean up what isn't working: rewriting vague copy, fixing mobile layout issues, improving page speed, or simplifying the path from search to contact.

## Why Work With Me

You work directly with the person writing the code and the copy. There's no account manager, no design-by-committee, and no drawn-out agency timeline. Most projects go from kickoff to launch in under two weeks.

I keep builds lightweight — static HTML where possible, minimal dependencies, and fast hosting. That matters for page speed, Core Web Vitals, and how your site performs in search. I also make sure the copy actually says something useful instead of filling space with filler paragraphs.

If you already have a Google Business Profile, I'll make sure the site connects to it properly and reinforces the same service areas, hours, and contact info.
